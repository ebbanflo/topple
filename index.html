      HOW TO PLAY

      Co-op. 1–4 players. Endless. Everyone types into the same tower. Every valid 5-letter word you land becomes a new floor — and every word must obey the current DECREE.

      THE DECREE

      A rule that every word must satisfy: use a letter → a letter pinned to a slot → banned letters → vowel counts → and stranger twists at HARD (a rare letter, matching first/last letters, "no E", "ends in K", a double letter, no vowels at all). It changes every 3 / 5 / 10 words — the host's call. LEVEL sets its difficulty: EASY / MED / HARD hold that band all game, RAMP climbs easy→hard and then stays there. Every decree is vetted at generation time to be clearable with words you'd actually recognise.

      LIVES

      Three each, capped at five. You lose one for a word that isn't real, a word that breaks the decree, or a word still visible in the tower (scrolled-off floors become playable again). The hunger bar drains constantly — if it empties, everyone bleeds a life and the combo resets. When every player is down, the tower falls.

      SCORING

      Each floor scores base × letter values × stage, inflated by your combo (every consecutive good word, team-wide). Numbers are meant to be absurd.

      REVIVE ✦

      Down a teammate? Anyone still standing can spend 5,000 points to start a rescue: solve one plain wordle and they're back with 2 lives. Starting a rescue pauses the hunger clock for the whole room — it's untimed — and it resumes with a fresh window once the rescue ends. Everyone watches the letters.

      BLESSINGS

      Every 10th floor, the whole team gets a bonus heart (including anyone at zero). And if the last five floors happen to spell T-O-W-E-R straight down any one column, the tower blesses you with another.
